Recruitment FAQ

Common questions

Is my application kept private?

Yes — applications are read only by the agency owner. Your name, photos and personal details are never shared with anyone, including other companions. If we don’t move forward, your details are deleted on request.

How much can I earn?

Earnings depend on hours, bookings and availability. Top performers earn well into 5 figures monthly. Full rates, commission split and weekly payout schedule are discussed privately on WhatsApp once we’ve had an initial chat.

Do I need experience?

No. We work with both experienced companions and first-timers. Attitude, presentation and reliability matter more than a CV. We’ll walk you through everything you need to know.

Do I have to use my real name or photos?

No. You choose a stage name and we use only professional photos taken or approved by you. Your legal name, ID and address are kept strictly internal.

Do I have to accept every booking?

No. Every booking comes to you on WhatsApp and you decide whether to accept it. You can also block-out days, set your own incall area, and pause bookings any time.

What about safety?

All clients are screened before a booking is sent to you. The dispatcher knows where you are and when you’re expected to finish. A driver is available for outcalls on request. You’re never sent into an unverified situation.

Do I need to be European?

Our brand focus is European companions, but we always consider exceptional applicants from any background. Send us your details on WhatsApp and we’ll be honest about fit.

I’m visiting London for a short tour — can I still apply?

Yes. We work with both London-based and touring companions. Tell us your dates and we’ll see if we can fill your calendar while you’re here.
